About Me

I am a political scientist at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Brazil, interested in comparative political behavior, public opinion, and democracy. My research combines cross-national survey data—including the AmericasBarometer, Afrobarometer, and World Values Survey—with historical, macroeconomic, and social indicators, using quantitative methods to investigate political attitudes and behavior across countries and over time.

In October 2025, I submitted a request to the faculty board of the Department of Social Sciences at the Federal University of Ceará to transfer to the University of International Integration of Afro-Brazilian Lusophony (Unilab), but the request was denied.
